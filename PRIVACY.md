# Privacy Policy — AI Translate

**Last updated:** 2026-04-01

## Data Collection

AI Translate does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal data to the developer or any third party.

## What is stored locally

- API keys you provide (stored in your browser's local storage via the Storage API)
- Your language preferences and translation settings

This data never leaves your browser except when making API calls to your chosen LLM provider.

## Third-party API calls

When you translate text, it is sent **only** to the LLM provider you selected:

- **Google Gemini** — `generativelanguage.googleapis.com`
- **Anthropic Claude** — `api.anthropic.com`
- **Azure OpenAI** — `*.openai.azure.com`

Your use of these services is subject to their respective privacy policies.
